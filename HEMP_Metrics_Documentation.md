# HEMP Program - Comprehensive Metrics Documentation

## Overview
The HEMP (Health Entrepreneurship & Mentorship Program) Dashboard tracks key performance indicators across five main activity types. This document provides a complete reference of all metrics measured across each program area.

---

## 1. Career Workshops (Career Development)

### Overview Metrics
- **Total Participants**: Total number of individuals who attended career workshops
- **Sessions Held**: Number of career development sessions delivered
- **Female Participation**: Percentage of female participants
- **Partner Organizations**: Number of partner organizations involved
- **Inclusion Reach**: Reach to vulnerable populations (persons with disabilities, refugees, etc.)
- **Quality Score**: Average satisfaction and quality rating of sessions

### Filters Available
- Year (2021-2026)
- Session Type (Format A, B, C, D)
- Partner Organization (63+ partners tracked)

### Key Data Points
- Participant demographics breakdown
- Partner engagement metrics
- Session type effectiveness comparison
- Year-over-year growth trends

---

## 2. Exposure Events (Career Exposure)

### Overview Metrics
- Participants exposed to industry professionals
- Event attendance and participation rates
- Industry partner engagement
- Female representation in events
- Networking connections made
- Follow-up conversion rates

### Key Tracking Areas
- Event type breakdown
- Geographic reach
- Partner organization participation
- Quality of interactions
- Post-event engagement

---

## 3. Internships

### Overview Metrics
- **Total Participants**: Number of participants in internship programs
- **Female Participation**: Gender distribution of interns
- **Employment Conversions**: Participants converted to permanent employment
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of interns who completed their placements
- **Satisfaction Score**: Intern satisfaction with the program
- **Inclusion Reach**: Participation from vulnerable populations

### Filters Available
- Year (2021-2026)
- Organization (partner organizations)
- Department (different organizational departments)
- Cohort (Cohort 1, 2, 3, etc.)
- Female (Yes/All)
- IDP (Internally Displaced Persons) - Yes/All
- PLWD (Persons Living with Disabilities) - Yes/All

### Key Metrics Tracked
- Duration of internship placements
- Skills gained during internship
- Salary/stipend information
- Employer satisfaction ratings
- Post-internship employment outcomes
- Network connections built

---

## 4. Student Immersive Experience (SIE)

### Overview Metrics
- **Participants Selected**: Number of students selected for SIE
- **Female Participation**: Gender breakdown of participants
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage completing the full immersion
- **Avg Exposure Score**: Average exposure and learning score
- **Satisfaction Score**: Student satisfaction rating
- **Inclusion Reach**: Participation from vulnerable populations

### Filters Available
- Year (2021-2026)
- Region (geographic regions)
- Country (specific countries)

### Key Data Points
- Pre/post program assessment scores
- Skill development tracking
- Mentor engagement levels
- Industry exposure metrics
- Leadership development indicators
- Career direction clarity post-SIE

---

## 5. Courses

### Overview Metrics
- **Total Enrolled**: Number of students enrolled in courses
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of students completing courses
- **Graduation Rate**: Percentage of graduates
- **Female Participation**: Gender distribution

- **Satisfaction**: Course and instructor satisfaction ratings
- **Inclusion Reach**: Reach to vulnerable populations

- Student engagement levels
