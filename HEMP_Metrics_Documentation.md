# HEMP Program - Comprehensive Metrics Documentation

## Overview
The HEMP (Health Entrepreneurship & Mentorship Program) Dashboard tracks key performance indicators across five main activity types. This document provides a complete reference of all metrics, visualizations, and data tracked across each program area.

---

## 1. Career Workshops (Career Development)

### Overview Metrics (Header Panel)
- **Total Participants**: Total cumulative participants across all career workshops
- **Sessions Held**: Total number of career development sessions delivered
- **Female Participation**: % female participants
- **Partner Organizations**: Number of active partner organizations

### Detailed Visualizations

#### 1. **Participants by Health Interest Area** (Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Distribution of participants across different health specializations
- **Data**: Breakdown by health interest areas (e.g., Public Health, Clinical Care, Health Tech, etc.)
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Understand which health sectors are attracting the most participant interest
- **Type**: Horizontal/Vertical Bar Chart

#### 2. **Participants by Academic Programmes** (Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Participant distribution across different academic programs/institutions
- **Data**: Count of participants by primary academic programme/university
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Track participation by academic institution and program popularity
- **Type**: Bar Chart with labels

#### 3. **Cumulative Growth** (Line Chart)
- **Description**: Participant growth trajectory against 2030 goals with session trends
- **Data**: 
  - Total participants over time
  - 2030 target line
  - Session count trend
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Monitor progress toward 2030 targets and session delivery trends
- **Type**: Dual-axis Line Chart (participants + sessions)

#### 4. **Inclusion Reach** (Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Diversity breakdown across disability and refugee status
- **Data**:
  - Participants with disabilities
  - Refugee/IDP participants
  - General population
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Track inclusion and reach to vulnerable populations
- **Type**: Stacked/Grouped Bar Chart

#### 5. **Rating Distribution** (Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Participant satisfaction score distribution
- **Data**: Distribution of satisfaction ratings (1-5 scale)
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Benchmark**: Average rating 4.0/5
- **Use**: Understand overall satisfaction levels
- **Type**: Histogram/Bar Chart

#### 6. **Quality Dimensions** (Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Multi-dimensional feedback ratings across key quality areas
- **Data**: Average ratings for:
  - Relevance (0-5 scale)
  - Quality of delivery (0-5 scale)
  - Usefulness (0-5 scale)
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Identify strengths and areas for improvement in workshop quality
- **Type**: Grouped Bar Chart

#### 7. **Recommendation & Confidence** (Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Net Promoter Score (NPS) and participant skill confidence assessment
- **Data**:
  - NPS score (0-10)
  - Post-workshop confidence level (0-5)
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Measure likelihood to recommend and skill confidence gain
- **Type**: Double Bar Chart

#### 8. **Satisfaction Trend** (Line Chart)
- **Description**: Quality metrics progression across different session formats
- **Data**: Average satisfaction ratings by:
  - Session Format (Format A, B, C, D)
  - Over time
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Compare effectiveness of different session formats and identify top performers
- **Type**: Multi-line Chart with legend

#### 9. **Partner Breakdown** (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- **Description**: Distribution of partner organizations by type
- **Data**: Count by organization type (e.g., Healthcare, Corporate, NGO, Academic, etc.)
- **Filter**: By Year
- **Use**: Understand partner ecosystem composition
- **Type**: Horizontal Bar Chart

#### 10. **Partner Directory** (Table/List)
- **Description**: Complete list of all active partner organizations
- **Data**: 63 active partner organizations with:
  - Organization name
  - Type
  - Number of sessions delivered
  - Participants engaged
- **Filter**: Searchable/Filterable list
- **Use**: Detailed reference of all partnerships

### Filters Available
- Year (2021-2026)
- Session Type (Format A, B, C, D)
- Partner Organization (63+ partners tracked)
- Health Interest Area

### Key Data Points Tracked
- Participant demographics (gender, disability status, refugee status)
- Partner engagement levels and preferences
- Session type effectiveness and popularity
- Year-over-year growth trends
- Quality and satisfaction metrics
- Program reach and inclusivity
