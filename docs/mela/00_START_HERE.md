## 📊 The 15-Sheet Workbook Structure

### Core Tracking (Must have)
1. **INDICATORS_MASTER** - Reference for all 57 indicators (one-time setup)
2. **AT_A_GLANCE_DATA** - High-level KPIs for dashboard
3. **OUTREACH_INTERVENTIONS** - Track all programme interventions
4. **VENTURES_PORTFOLIO** - Central venture database (96 ventures)
5. **GESI_TRACKING** - Gender & equity disaggregation for all records

### Outcome Tracking
6. **EMPLOYMENT_OUTCOMES** - Employment/internship placements
7. **ENTREPRENEURSHIP_OUTCOMES** - Post-cohort venture metrics
8. **FURTHER_EDUCATION_OUTCOMES** - Knowledge & career progression

### Research & Partnerships
9. **RESEARCH_PARTNERSHIPS** - Formal research partnerships (12 active)
10. **RESEARCH_INTERVENTIONS** - Individual research projects

### Leadership Programmes
11. **FELLOWSHIPS_DATA** - HECO fellows tracking (20 fellows)
12. **HACKATHONS_DATA** - HECO hackathon events (6+ per year)
13. **FUNDING_TRACKING** - Funding initiatives & grants

### Quality & Analysis
14. **DATA_QUALITY_FLAGS** - Track data quality issues for 7 flagged indicators
15. **TREND_ANALYSIS** - Year-over-year trends (2022-2026)

## 🎯 What Gets Measured

### At the Intervention Level
- What activities are delivered (hackathons, masterclasses, mentorship, field trips, etc.)
- Who attends (gender, youth, IDP/refugee, PWD status)
- How satisfied they are
- What skills they gain

### At the Individual Level
- Knowledge/skill improvement
- Confidence & career clarity gained
- Employment status & quality (wage, enterprise, freelance)
- Further education progression

### At the Venture Level
- Business readiness (business plan, MVP, go-to-market strategy)
- Financial metrics (funding raised, revenue, jobs created)
- Growth stage & trajectory
- Market traction (customers, partnerships, user growth)
- Women-led vs. male-led comparison

### At the Programme Level
- Aggregate reach & participation metrics
- GESI representation across all programmes
- Total funding mobilised
- Total jobs created
- Revenue generated by portfolio
- Health system adoption
- Policy influence

### At the Ecosystem Level
- Alumni activity as mentors/investors
- New ventures founded by alumni
- Publications & knowledge contribution
- Long-term venture sustainability (3-year, 5-year survival rates)
- Lives impacted & health outcomes improved

## 🚨 7 Flagged Indicators Requiring Extra Care

These indicators need special attention for data quality:

1. **INT-06** (Funding facilitated) - Attribution clarity needed
2. **INT-14** (Non-financial resources value) - Valuation methodology
3. **IMM-11** (MVP iterations) - Definition consistency
4. **INT-O-07** (Product-market fit) - Assessment framework
5. **INT-O-14** (Gender gap) - Small sample size issues
6. **LTO-02** (Lives impacted) - Attribution methodology
7. **LTO-10** (Economic value) - Calculation methodology

See DATA_QUALITY_FLAGS sheet in workbook for mitigation strategies.
